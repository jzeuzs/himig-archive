Globally, nearly 1 in 3 women, roughly 840 million, have experienced physical or sexual violence at least once in their lifetime. In addition, studies show that about 41% of women worldwide have experienced online harassment, highlighting how digital spaces often mirror real-world discrimination. Instead, it should promote reflection from everyone. No matter one’s status or platform, every person has the responsibility to communicate respectfully. While freedom of expression is essential in a democratic society, it should never be used as an excuse for speech that diminishes others. As National Women’s Month continues, let it remind us of the work that still lies ahead. Celebrating women goes beyond symbolic gestures or annual observances. To celebrate women is to defend their dignity, stand guard over their worth, and honor their contributions every single day of the year.
